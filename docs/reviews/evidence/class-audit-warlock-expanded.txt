Warlock

A Patron supplies Warlock power, a Pact is the type or manifestation of its agreement, and Invocations are additional terms attached to that Pact. This selected modular design is under active testing alongside coherent native classes; it is not a source edition candidate or a finalized architecture decision.

Rules status: selected for playtest; subject to change · Alpha 0.3.1

Pact of the Blade
Level 1+
Pact of the Blade

Manifest the agreement in a weapon. Use a Bonus Action to conjure or bind an eligible melee weapon; gain proficiency, Charisma option for attacks and damage, and Warlock focus use; choose ordinary, Necrotic, Psychic, or Radiant damage per attack. Follow the host's modern bond and ending restrictions.

Pact of the Chain
Level 1+
Pact of the Chain

Know Find Familiar without spending an ordinary known-spell selection. Cast it as a Magic action without a slot while retaining components, or as a ritual. Use host statblocks for Imp, Pseudodragon, Quasit, Skeleton, Sphinx of Wonder, Sprite, Venomous Snake, or ordinary forms. Familiar shares initiative but takes its own turn. Forgo one of your attacks during your Attack action to command one familiar Attack action in that shared round.

Pact of the Talisman
Level 1+
Pact of the Talisman

One active wearable or transferable talisman. Untrained Guidance adds 1d4 to an unproficient skill ability check without spending charges. Fortunate Correction spends one charge after a failed ability check to add 1d4. They cannot both enhance one check, and neither applies to attacks or saving throws. Charges equal Warlock proficiency bonus and recover on Long Rest; replacing a lost talisman takes a one-hour ceremony and does not restore charges.

Full requirements and mechanics
Open full rule
Pact of the Tome
Level 1+
Pact of the Tome

Conjure a Book of Shadows at the end of a Short or Long Rest. While carrying it, gain three extra cantrips from any class list and two level-1 Ritual spells, with the book as a focus. For this alpha, starter Ritual spells are ritual-only unless separately known; replacement does not recharge limited uses. Book of Ancient Secrets separately supports additional rituals.

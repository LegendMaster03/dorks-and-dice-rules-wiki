Warlock

A Patron supplies Warlock power, a Pact is the type or manifestation of its agreement, and Invocations are additional terms attached to that Pact. This selected modular design is under active testing alongside coherent native classes; it is not a source edition candidate or a finalized architecture decision.

Pact Magic
Pact Magic

Charisma is the spellcasting ability. Use the progression table for known spells, cantrips, Pact slots and slot level. All Pact slots share one level, recover on Short or Long Rest, and cast eligible known Warlock spells without daily preparation. When gaining a class level, replace up to one ordinary cantrip and one ordinary leveled spell. Patron, Pact and Mystic Arcanum grants do not consume ordinary known-spell selections.

Eldritch Blast
Eldritch Blast

Automatically know Eldritch Blast in addition to ordinary cantrips. Use the host game's Warlock cantrip; the beam count is 1 at level 1, 2 at 5, 3 at 11, and 4 at 17, resolved separately. It qualifies for Invocations but does not gain historical 3.5e blast dice.

Patron Choice
Otherworldly Patron

Choose the ongoing source of supernatural power at level 1; the selected alpha Patron is Fiend. A Patron may be inherited, involuntary, concealed or unknown by name, without direct contact or a literal contract. Do not gain a second Patron at level 3.

Pact Choice
Pact Boon

Choose one free Pact at level 1 from Blade, Chain, Tome, and Talisman. It represents the manifestation or terms of a deal. The first Pact does not consume an Invocation selection. Additional Pacts are out of scope.

Eldritch Invocations
Eldritch Invocations

Gain one elective at level 1, increasing to 12 at level 20 under the selected hybrid curve. Each Invocation costs one elective, attaches to one actual Pact and requires acceptance by that Pact's Patron. Meet all prerequisites. On Warlock level-up you may replace one option with an eligible alternative. Severance suspends attached Invocations without automatic refund; restoration or transfer requires revalidation.

Magical Cunning
Magical Cunning

Spend one minute in an esoteric rite to recover expended Pact Magic slots up to half the slot maximum, rounded up; one at levels 2–10, two at levels 11–19. The rite is available once per Long Rest and does not replenish other resources. Eldritch Master upgrades the same rite.

Damage Reduction
Damage Reduction
Damage Reduction

Reduce Bludgeoning, Piercing and Slashing damage from weapon or natural attacks by 1 at level 3; by 2 at 7, 3 at 11, 4 at 15, 5 at 19. Cold iron bypasses this mitigation. Reduce each qualifying damage component once, before ordinary half-damage Resistance. Do not reduce separately typed elemental or other damage riders.

Ability Score Improvement

At Warlock levels 4, 8, 12, 16, and 19, increase one ability score by 2 or two by 1, with the ordinary host-game increase ceiling for this alpha; an eligible feat may be chosen under the table's rules. The level-19 grant is ordinary, not an automatic Epic Boon.

Deceive Item
Deceive Item

For eligible magic-item-use competency checks, treat the d20 as 10 even under threat or distraction, then apply modifiers. Use Charisma and proficiency where trained. It does not grant automatic success or apply to unrelated attacks, saving throws or skills; omit testing if the host has no such competency check.

Deepened Pact

Energy Resistance
Energy Resistance

Choose two damage types from Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning and Thunder. Reduce each selected damage type by 5 per instance at level 10, increasing to 10 at level 20. This is flat damage mitigation before ordinary Resistance, not damage-halving Resistance. It is distinct from the retired Fiend level-10 feature.

Mystic Arcanum (6th)

Choose one 6th-level Warlock spell and cast it once without a slot per Long Rest. It is separate from Pact Magic and other Arcanum levels. You may replace one Arcanum spell when gaining a Warlock level, with another of the same spell level.

Imbue Item
Imbue Item

When qualified to create a magic item, substitute a magic-item-use check for a missing prerequisite spell: DC 15 + spell level for arcane or DC 25 + spell level for divine. Other requirements and costs remain. A failure does not complete the item or consume its creation payment; do not retry until next Warlock level. Skip if host has no crafting/check procedure.
